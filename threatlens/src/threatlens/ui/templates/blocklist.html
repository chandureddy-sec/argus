How blocking works

  1. Monitor. A new block is recorded only. Nothing pulls it.
  2. Enforce. {% if policy.require_second_approver %}Someone requests it; a different admin approves.{% else %}An admin enforces it.{% endif %} It then appears in the feed your firewall pulls.
  3. Expire or lift. Every block ends on its own; lifting ends it now and the firewall drops it on its next pull.

Checked twice, when you block and on every export: our own IPs and domains, ReconX assets, private and reserved ranges, public DNS resolvers, CDN edges and popular domains are never blocked.
